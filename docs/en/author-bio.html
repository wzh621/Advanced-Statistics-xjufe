Author Biography

Zhihao Wang is an associate professor and master’s supervisor. He holds a PhD in Statistics from Renmin University of China, serves as Head of the Department of Statistics in the School of Statistics and Data Science at Xinjiang University of Finance and Economics, and is also a specially appointed supervisor at Kyrgyz Economic University.

Research and Teaching

His research interests include nonparametric statistical models, quantile regression, functional data analysis, high-dimensional variable selection, and tourism statistics. He has led or participated in five national projects, five provincial or ministerial projects, one teaching-reform project, and more than ten departmental or bureau-level projects.

His teaching connects statistical theory, computational tools, and practical questions. Reproducible code is used to support conceptual understanding, while the paired Chinese and English handouts support both classroom teaching and independent review.

Academic Work

He has published more than ten papers in journals including Science China Mathematics, Statistical Research, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series A, Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, Statistica Sinica, Biometrical Journal, Statistical Papers, Journal of Systems Science and Mathematical Sciences, and Journal of Applied Statistics and Management.
